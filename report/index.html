Where we really are

Progress exists. The units matter.

These numbers answer different questions. Keeping them separate prevents another smoke-test “victory” from being mistaken for general Scrabble ability.

Contaminated smoke100%Memorized five public boards. Proof that 4B capacity is sufficient to memorize—not to generalize.

Supplied-plan ranking46%V4 diagnostic: choose the best among provided legal candidates. A real but scaffolded skill.

Free playBoard + rack → moveThe benchmark goal. Must be legal and exactly optimal without hidden candidates.
Pass@kAny success in k triesUseful for exploration, but never comparable to single-answer accuracy.
Candidate rankingChoose from a listMeasures judgment after search has already reduced the problem.
Slot recallFind geometry onlyDoes not choose a word or prove that the eventual move is legal.
Permanent metric rule: “50% accuracy” means 50% exact-optimal, legal, single-answer moves on whole-game-held-out boards. Every proxy must keep its own name.

V33
Scale failed21,874 records · 2,735 steps

A much lower loss did not make a better player.

We enlarged the corpus, added dense coordinates and hard negatives, and drove weighted loss down by 95.7%. On the matched 20-board test, V3 and V2 each produced only one legal opening move; every non-empty board failed.

KeptDense versus sparse serialization scored identically; representation text format was not the main bottleneck.
Never repeatDo not use falling teacher-forced loss as evidence that free board search improved.

4.33
Recovery SFTOn-policy hard negatives

Recovery changed the success set instead of accumulating skill.

V4.3 found legal moves on different boards than V4.2, but the overlap between their pass@16 success sets was zero. Earlier success was not retained; the new checkpoint was complementary, not generally better.

V88
Causal auditQuackle-certified · full legal space

The failure is coupled, not one bad coordinate.

Correcting row, column, or even direction did not make the remaining word adapt. Supplying a complete correct plan did: V4.2 then executed legal moves 78.26% of the time and optimal moves 69.57% of the time.

12.50%legal with correct location + direction
69.57%optimal with complete plan
100 / 100Quackle score agreement

A six-model frozen ranker over 117,465 legal plans reached only 6.67% exact top-1 and 47.98% point ratio. Length normalization improved top-1 to 13%, but a trivial face-value heuristic reached 16.48%.

A parallel 4.2M-parameter board-native decoder told the same story: canonical and set-valued losses fell, while pass@32 legality stayed near 5%. A lexicon trie guaranteed dictionary prefixes but reduced legal pass@32, because valid words can still collide, disconnect, or violate cross-words.

KeptComplete-plan execution is strong. Word, geometry, rack use, crosses, and score must be solved jointly.
Never repeatA learned ranker must beat trivial heuristics before receiving more training budget.

V1111
Pointer signal, cross failureAtomic square glyphs · staged legality curriculum

Atomic pointers grounded simple boards, then failed at coupled crosses.

Tying each board square to one existing vocabulary glyph produced real transfer: 93% opening legality and 44% one-anchor legality. Controlled-cross legality reached only 3/40. On a fresh adversarial 200-board gate the best cross checkpoint gained just one percentage point over the pre-cross model.

93%opening legality retained
44%one-anchor legality
3 / 40controlled-cross legality

A 400-board true-cross control then regressed from 10.75% to 6.50% legal under ordinary SFT. Exact eight-positive marginal-likelihood training collapsed the output grammar completely: 0/400 parseable and 0/400 legal after about $2.8 total paid V11 spend.

KeptAtomic square pointers remove a real representation burden on openings and simple anchors.
Never repeatDo not continue unaided full-board SFT, verifier RL, or multi-positive MML from this objective.
V1212
Off-target experimentCPU pairwise ranker · complete legal space

The ranker worked, but it tested the wrong capability.

A symbolic generator enumerated every legal move while scores stayed in evaluator-only files. A 15-feature linear ranker trained on 240 boards and 103,042 legal candidates. It passed validation, then reached 83% exact top-1 on the frozen 300-game test with no whole-game overlap.

83.00%frozen 300-board top-1
97.60%frozen-test point ratio
100%optimal recall@32

The model was frozen before one official confirmation. On the 100 public benchmark positions it reached 74% exact-optimal top-1 and 93.66% point ratio; face-value reached 15.2%. Official top-1 remained 60% on boards with more than 512 legal candidates.

Kept separatelyLegal search plus learned value ranking is a viable hybrid diagnostic and its artifacts remain reproducible.
Does not countThe symbolic solver already performed candidate discovery. The 74% result must never be described as progress toward the unaided 50% goal.

Lab rules

How we avoid repeating ourselves.

These are gates, not suggestions. A new run should explicitly state which rule it satisfies.

Freeze the meaning of 50%.

Single answer, exactly optimal, legal, whole-game held-out. Pass@k, slot recall, and candidate ranking remain secondary metrics.

Split by whole game first.

No board, rack hash, game seed, augmentation sibling, or benchmark position may cross train, validation, selection, and final test.

Behavior beats loss.

Checkpoint selection uses frozen legality, optimality, score ratio, and coverage—not training loss or teacher-forced perplexity.

Beat the cheap baseline.

A learned ranker must outperform random, longest-word, face-value, and frequency heuristics on the same full candidate space.

Test one causal claim.

Every experiment changes one bottleneck and includes an intervention or control that could falsify the story.

Audit labels independently.

Quackle parity, all tied optima, set-valued targets, and evaluator regression tests happen before GPU spend.

Regression-test retained skills.

Paired boards must show whether a new objective adds capability or merely changes which boards happen to succeed.

RL needs reward diversity.

No online RL until enough rollout groups contain both legal successes and informative failures; all-zero groups teach nothing.

Preflight the immutable bundle.

Verify the lexicon, tokenizer/chat template, token ceiling, one full example, upload paths, and evaluator imports before starting billed training.

Checkpoint before evaluation.

Upload completed training artifacts before long gates, log cost and hashes, resume evaluation without retraining, and stop idle Colab/HF resources.

Current recommendation

Return to the unaided generation target.

Do not choose the next experiment by proxy

The next experiment must evaluate a model that receives only the board and rack and emits one move. No solver candidates, plans, coordinates, scores, or hidden search artifacts may enter the inference path.

Decision required: V12 is retained only as an explicitly separate diagnostic. Do not infer a V13 direction from it or run another benchmark until the target-aligned hypothesis is agreed with the user.

Metric caveats preserved for future readers

The 100% smoke result is contaminated. V2 exact optimal counts include a maximum of two attempts. V4’s 46% result ranks supplied candidates. V4.1’s 44% ranks complete supplied plans. V4.2’s 5% is pass@32, not greedy accuracy. V8’s 13% is a post-hoc length-normalized full-candidate ranker. V9’s 25.25% ranks slots inside a teacher-generated shortlist with 100% optimal coverage by construction. V10 used only five frozen boards per checkpoint. V11’s opening and one-anchor results are restricted curriculum gates. V12’s 74% is an end-to-end single-answer hybrid result whose internal symbolic component enumerates the complete legal space; it is not unaided LLM generation.
